The BuilderX Build Challenge has eight levels, from 10 minutes to one month. Every level ends with a real creation you can share and keep improving. No programming background needed — we use free tools throughout.

| Level | Estimated time | What you will finish with | Link |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🏁 Level 0 · Your First “Creation” | 10 min | An AI conversation you have revised | [Start](/en/00-first-build/) |
| 🌐 Level 1 · Your First Webpage | 15 min | A one-page site you can open and share | [Start](/en/01-first-webpage/) |
| 🛠️ Level 2 · Your First Tool | 30 min | A browser tool that solves one small problem | [Start](/en/02-first-tool/) |
| 🤖 Level 3 · Your First AI Bot | 1 hour | A bot that answers questions on a chosen topic | [Start](/en/03-first-bot/) |
| 📱 Level 4 · Your First Mobile App | Half a day | A web app designed for use on a phone | [Start](/en/04-first-miniapp/) |
| ⚡ Level 5 · Your First Automation | Half a day | A workflow that handles one repetitive task | [Start](/en/05-first-automation/) |
| 💰 Level 6 · Your First Paid Product | A weekend | A plan for pricing, payment, and delivery | [Start](/en/06-first-paid-product/) |
| 🌱 Level 7 · Your First 100 Users | One month | An action plan for finding users and collecting feedback | [Start](/en/07-first-100-users/) |

## How to start

1. Open [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com) (free, works in your browser)
2. Start with [Level 0](/en/00-first-build/) and follow the steps
3. After each level, share your creation in the BuilderX community (Discord / WeChat groups)
